Privacy Policy

Meals: Nutrition & Calories

Last updated 8 October 2026

In short: your food diary stays on your iPhone. Meals has no account, no advertising and no tracking, and I don't collect, see or sell your data. Only two things ever leave your iPhone, and neither reaches me: a barcode number when you scan a product, so its nutrition values can be looked up, and, where Meals offers its closer look, the photo of a plate you scan, which Apple's Private Cloud Compute checks and then deletes.

Meals is made by Boyan Tasevski, an independent developer based in Switzerland ("I" in this policy). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Meals app on iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

Everything you put into Meals is stored only on your iPhone:

  • your profile: height, weight, age, sex, goal weight, pace, activity level and daily targets;
  • your food diary: meals, foods, amounts, nutrition values, nutrition grades and water;
  • the meal photos you take or choose.

The Meals widgets on your Home Screen and Lock Screen read the same data from your iPhone. I don't run a server, and I have no copy of any of it.

Camera and photos

Meals uses the camera only while you're scanning. Photos, nutrition labels and barcodes are read on your iPhone using Apple's on-device frameworks, and where Meals offers it, a plate photo can also get a closer look in Apple's Private Cloud Compute, as described below. When you choose a photo from your library, Meals receives only the photo you choose, not the rest of your library. A meal's photo is kept with that meal on your iPhone until you delete the meal.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional, and you choose exactly what to share. If you connect it:

  • Meals reads your height, weight, date of birth, sex and active energy, to set up your targets, adjust them on days you move more, and estimate when you'll reach your goal weight.
  • Meals writes the energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat and water you log, with each meal under its name. When you edit or delete an entry, Meals updates Apple Health to match.

Health data is used only to provide these features on your iPhone. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with anyone, and Meals never copies it to iCloud or any other online service. You can change what Meals can read or write at any time in the Health app or in Settings.

Barcode lookups

When you scan a barcode, Meals sends only that barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free, non-profit food database, to get the product's name and nutrition values. Nothing about you is sent with it. As with any website, their server sees your device's internet address in order to answer; see the Open Food Facts privacy policy. The result is saved on your iPhone, so scanning the same product again doesn't need the internet.

Plate scans and Apple's Private Cloud Compute

When you scan a plate of food, Meals first estimates it on your iPhone. Where Meals offers its closer look and you're online, Meals then sends that photo to Apple's Private Cloud Compute, the secure cloud Apple uses for Apple Intelligence, for a closer look. Only the photo and the app's instructions are sent, nothing about you. Versions of Meals without the closer look keep plate photos on your iPhone only.

Apple states that Private Cloud Compute uses the data only to answer the request, that no one can access it, not even Apple staff, and that nothing is kept once the answer comes back. You can read Apple's description of Private Cloud Compute. I never receive your photo.

If the closer look finds a noticeably different total, Meals updates the meal and marks it "Updated after a closer look". Anything you changed by hand is never overwritten. If you're offline, the check is skipped and the estimate from your iPhone stays. Wherever the closer look is offered, you can turn it off in Profile → Privacy & Support ("Closer look in Apple's cloud"); with it off, plate photos stay on your iPhone.

Everything else happens on your iPhone

Reading a nutrition label, working out calories and nutrition grades, and calculating your targets all happen on your iPhone. The nutrition values for common foods are built into the app, so no lookup is needed.

What Meals doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising code.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data.

Purchases

Meals is a paid download sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles the payment, and I never see your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Crash reports from Apple

If you've chosen in your iPhone's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and app statistics, such as how many people installed the app. These never include your food diary or Health data. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Backups

If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or to a computer, Meals' data is included in that backup, as with any app. The backup belongs to your Apple Account or your computer, and I can't access it.

Deleting your data

  • Delete any meal or glass of water in the app, and Meals removes it from Apple Health too.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone, including its widgets.
  • Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app (your profile → Apps → Meals).

Children

Meals is not intended for children under 16.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your iPhone, I hold no personal data about you that could be accessed, corrected or deleted. If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply, and delete them once they're no longer needed. You have rights under Swiss data protection law and, if you live in the EU, under the GDPR, including the right to complain to a data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version of Meals sends any new kind of data off your iPhone, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
